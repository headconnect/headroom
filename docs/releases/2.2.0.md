# Rations 2.2.0

- Optional menu bar countdowns when a quota reaches 100%: `04:37` for hours
  and minutes, or `3d 17:29` for longer waits. Enable them separately for session
  and weekly usage in Settings, with per-account overrides. Copilot supports
  the same option for its monthly quota. Countdowns update locally every minute
  without extra API requests and are off by default.
- Claude's popover now shows **Weekly · Fable** when Anthropic reports that
  allowance, including its percentage used and reset countdown. The percentage
  measures the Fable allowance itself. Model-specific limits stay out of the
  menu bar, even when a session or weekly limit is missing.

Existing accounts and appearance settings are preserved. If a quota has no
reset time, its percentage remains visible; after the reset time, `00:00` stays
until fresh usage arrives.
